Grand Piano

The largest and most expensive type of piano, known for its rich, resonant sound.

A grand piano can have over 200 strings!

🎻

Upright Piano

The most common type of piano, with strings running vertically.

Upright pianos can have up to 230 strings!

💻

Digital Piano

Modern electronic pianos that simulate acoustic piano sounds.

Digital pianos can produce 128 voices simultaneously!

🎼

Piano Harp

A rare hybrid instrument combining features of both pianos and harps.

The piano harp has 88 keys and 4,000+ strings!

Piano Facts

🎹 Keys

A standard piano has 88 keys, spanning seven and a half octaves.

🎼 History

The piano was invented by Bartolomeo Cristofori in Italy around 1700.

🎶 Strings

Each key on a piano controls one or more strings that vibrate to produce sound.

Famous Pianists

🎹 Ludwig van Beethoven

German composer and pianist known for his powerful and emotional piano works.

🎹 Frédéric Chopin

Polish composer and virtuoso pianist, known as the "Poet of the Piano".

🎹 Clara Schumann

German pianist and composer, one of the most celebrated pianists of the 19th century.
